## ☁️ A little about me

<a href="https://tenor.com/view/killua-zoldyck-cute-smile-anime-gif-18358453"><img align="right" src="assets/killua-smile.gif" width="190" alt="A short animation of Killua from Hunter × Hunter" /></a>

Hi, I'm **Alex Wang**. I study cancer across experiments and computation, using **tumor omics, single-cell and spatial analysis, functional genomics, and in vivo models** to identify and validate therapeutic targets.

I also have experience in **DNA nanomedicine and targeted drug delivery**. This is where I share reproducible analyses and the questions behind them.

⚡ Away from the data: **Hunter × Hunter**, especially **Killua**.

<br clear="right" />

## 🔎 Research quests

- **Decode tumors** — large-scale tumor transcriptomics and clinical-data integration.
- **Zoom into cells** — single-cell analysis of tumor heterogeneity and the microenvironment.
- **Test the mechanism** — functional validation with CRISPR screening, molecular biology, and tumor models.
- **Explore delivery** — nanomedicine-based therapeutic delivery.
