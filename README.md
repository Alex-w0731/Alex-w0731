# Hi, I'm Zhongliang (Alex) Wang

I am an experimental-computational cancer researcher interested in using tumor omics, single-cell and spatial analysis, functional genomics, and in vivo models to identify and validate therapeutic targets. I also have experience in DNA nanomedicine and targeted drug-delivery systems.

## Research focus

- Large-scale tumor transcriptomics and clinical-data integration
- Single-cell analysis of tumor heterogeneity and the microenvironment
- Functional validation with CRISPR screening, molecular biology, and tumor models
- Nanomedicine-based therapeutic delivery

## Technical toolkit

**Computational:** R, Python, bulk RNA-seq, scRNA-seq, ChIP-seq, Seurat, Scanpy, CellChat, CytoTRACE2, Palantir, pySCENIC, pathway enrichment, survival analysis, and reproducible visualization

**Experimental:** genome-wide CRISPR-Cas9 screening, qRT-PCR, Western blotting, Co-IP, flow cytometry, mammalian cell culture, xenograft models, H&E, IHC, immunofluorescence, confocal microscopy, and quantitative pathology-image analysis

## Reproducible portfolio

- [TCGA-LUAD survival analysis](https://github.com/Alex-w0731/tcga-luad-survival-analysis) - programmatic cBioPortal retrieval, cohort construction, Cox modeling, FDR correction, and survival visualization
- [Single-cell melanoma analysis](https://github.com/Alex-w0731/single-cell-melanoma-analysis) - memory-conscious processing of GSE72056, Scanpy dimensionality reduction, Leiden clustering, and marker analysis
