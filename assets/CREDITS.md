## The Curiosity Lab (current theme)

- **Character artwork:** original AI-generated Killua scientist fan art made for Alex's profile with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool. The prompt specified silver hair, blue eyes, a happy expression, lab goggles, a glowing flask, and electricity on a transparent background. [Generation prompt](../motion/character-prompt.txt) · [Character source](../motion/killua-scientist.png).
- **Motion design:** custom six-second HyperFrames / GSAP composition: floating and tilting character, orbiting electrons, rising bubbles, a pulsing lightning bolt, and a traveling research signal. This is original motion design using the character illustration, not an excerpt from the anime. [Editable composition](../motion/index.html) · [Reproduction notes](../motion/README.md).
- **Accessibility:** the header includes a static poster for reduced-motion preference. No full-screen flashing; text stays readable throughout the loop.
