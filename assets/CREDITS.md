# Artwork & animation

## Mission control (current theme)

- **Graphics:** every panel on the profile (hero, section headers, research loop, project tiles, feature banners, toolkit matrix, radar, footer and buttons) is an original SVG generated by the scripts in [`tools/profile_assets`](../tools/profile_assets/). `python3 tools/profile_assets/build.py` rebuilds them and `--check` verifies that the committed files are current. No third-party artwork, icon sets, web fonts or external services are used.
- **Motion:** CSS animations inside the SVG files. They stop under `prefers-reduced-motion`, and every graphic is a complete picture without animation.
- **What the drawings are:** schematics. Cell positions, curves, heat-map tiles and radar blips carry no measurements; the only number shown is the size of the research catalogue.
- **Alternate hero:** [`assets/hud/hero-terminal.svg`](hud/hero-terminal.svg) is a terminal-style variant. Point the first image in `README.md` at it to switch.

## The Curiosity Lab (previous theme, now the easter egg at the bottom of the profile)

- **Character artwork:** original AI-generated Killua scientist fan art made for Alex's profile with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool. The prompt specified silver hair, blue eyes, a happy expression, lab goggles, a glowing flask, and electricity on a transparent background. [Generation prompt](../motion/character-prompt.txt) · [Character source](../motion/killua-scientist.png).
- **Motion design:** custom six-second HyperFrames / GSAP composition: floating and tilting character, orbiting electrons, rising bubbles, a pulsing lightning bolt, and a traveling research signal. This is original motion design using the character illustration, not an excerpt from the anime. [Editable composition](../motion/index.html) · [Reproduction notes](../motion/README.md).
- **Accessibility:** the header includes a static poster for reduced-motion preference. No full-screen flashing; text stays readable throughout the loop.
- **Diagrams:** custom SVG illustrations and badges. Molecular and project graphics are decorative schematics, not experimental results or quantitative plots.
- **Character rights:** Killua Zoldyck is from *Hunter × Hunter*, created by Yoshihiro Togashi. Character and anime rights belong to their respective owners. This personal fan-themed profile has no official affiliation.
