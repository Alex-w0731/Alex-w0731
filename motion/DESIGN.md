# The Curiosity Lab

## Style Prompt
A personal scientist's electric playground, built around an original chibi Killua laboratory mascot. Combine precise scientific diagram geometry with an exuberant character and generous editorial typography. The dark blue canvas refers to late-night scientific work; cyan refers to Killua's electricity; lavender connects to the existing avatar and profile. Graphics suggest molecules and signals without claiming to show measurements. A scientist with a playful mind, always curious.

## Colors
- Midnight #0D1424: canvas.
- Ice #EAF3FF: primary text.
- Electric cyan #63E4F2: traces and accents.
- Lavender #BBA2FF: secondary orbit and titles.
- Slate blue #9DACC6: secondary text. Geometric rules use #243850.

## Typography
- Avenir Next, 800: expressive display voice.
- Menlo, 400: precise scientific labels.

## Motion
One six-second continuous scene. All meaningful copy remains legible throughout. Killua floats and gently tilts, molecule electrons orbit, bubbles rise, and a signal moves around a research loop. First and last states match. Authored at 1100 × 420; GIF export at 880 × 336 and 12 fps. Provide a static poster for reduced-motion preference.

## What NOT to Do
- No artificial publication counts, skill percentages, fake experiments, or invented credentials.
- No third-party stats badges, trackers, or external animation hosts.
- No entire-screen flashes or perpetually disappearing titles.
- No generic grid behind everything; diagram geometry belongs near the character.
- No extra anime characters; Killua is the focal mascot.
